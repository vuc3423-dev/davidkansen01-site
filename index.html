Editing process

Three steps, with a review round in the middle so the final cut matches what you had in mind.

  1. Agree on the brief

    Confirm the publishing platform, target length, deadline and a few reference videos whose style you like. Send the raw footage through a shared folder.

  2. Review the first cut

    Watch the first version and leave feedback with timestamps, for example "02:15 – cut this pause". The editor adjusts pacing, audio, text and captions.

  3. Get files ready to publish

    Approve the final cut and receive your files in the agreed aspect ratio and resolution, plus caption files if you ordered them.

What makes an edit work

Most viewers decide in the first few seconds whether to keep watching. A good edit opens with the most interesting moment, removes repeated or slow parts, and keeps the picture changing often enough to hold attention without feeling rushed.

Sound matters as much as the picture. Clear speech at a steady level, music that sits under the voice and sound effects that match what is on screen make a video feel professional even when it was filmed on a phone.

Captions help too: many people watch with the sound off, and captions make a video easier to follow for everyone. Read more in our frequently asked questions or learn how Frame Studio works.
